# Setting Up a New Mac

Moving to a new Mac takes a few minutes, and there is nothing to remember.

1. Open [apps.amansinghphotos.com](https://apps.amansinghphotos.com), download the apps you want, and
   drag each one to your Applications folder.
2. Open an app. If you have used it before, it offers to bring your earlier work across: choose your
   settings folder or an export file.
3. Allow what it asks for. Its first window shows each permission and opens the right switch for you.
4. If the app uses Claude, paste your key once when it asks. Your other apps find it by themselves.
