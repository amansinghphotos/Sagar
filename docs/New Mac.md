# Setting Up a New Mac

Moving to a new Mac takes a few minutes. There's nothing to remember.

1. Go to [apps.amansinghphotos.com](https://apps.amansinghphotos.com) and download the apps you want.
   Drag each one to your Applications folder.
2. Open an app. If you've used it before, it offers to bring your work across. Just choose your
   settings folder or an export file.
3. Allow what it asks for. Its first window shows each permission and opens the right switch.
4. If the app uses Claude, paste your key once when it asks. Your other apps find it by themselves.
