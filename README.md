# Sagar

Sagar (ਸਾਗਰ, the ocean) is a small collection of apps for the Mac. Each one is made to do a single
everyday thing well, and to feel at home on your Mac.

The first app is on its way. When it is ready, you will find it here:

**[apps.amansinghphotos.com](https://apps.amansinghphotos.com/)**

## What to Expect

- **Made for the Mac.** Each app needs macOS 27, and looks and behaves the way a Mac app should.
- **Yours to keep.** Your settings and your work stay in plain files on your own Mac.
- **Kept up to date, on your terms.** An app tells you when a new version is ready and what is new
  in it. You choose when to take it.
- **Happy offline.** Unless an app says otherwise, it needs no connection.

## Getting an App

Download it from the page above and drag it to your Applications folder. The first time you open it,
your Mac asks whether you are sure: choose Open.

Setting up a new Mac? [This short page](docs/New%20Mac.md) walks you through it.

## For the Curious

Every app here is made the same careful way. What it must do is written down first, each piece is
tested before it is added, and nothing is released until it has been tried. If you would like to see
how, [the plan](SPEC.md) is the place to start.

The code is here to be read. It is not set up to take contributions.
