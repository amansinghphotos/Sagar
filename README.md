# Sagar

Sagar (ਸਾਗਰ, the ocean) is a home for small Mac apps. Each one does a single everyday thing, and
does it well.

The first app is on its way. When it's ready, you'll find it here:

**[apps.amansinghphotos.com](https://apps.amansinghphotos.com/)**

## What to Expect

- **Made for the Mac.** Every app looks and works the way a Mac app should. You'll need macOS 27.
- **Yours to keep.** Your settings and your work stay on your own Mac.
- **Updates when you want them.** Each app tells you when there's an update and what's new. You
  choose when to take it.
- **Happy offline.** No internet needed, unless an app tells you otherwise.

## Getting an App

Download it from the page above. Then drag it to your Applications folder. The first time you open
it, your Mac asks if you're sure. Choose Open.

Setting up a new Mac? [This short page](docs/New%20Mac.md) walks you through it.

## For the Curious

Every app here is made the same careful way. What it must do is written down first. Each piece is
tested before it goes in. Nothing is released until it's been tried. Want to see how?
[The plan](SPEC.md) is the place to start.
