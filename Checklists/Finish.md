# Finishing

Read every line at the end of every session.

- [ ] My branch is merged, or its pull request is open and says what it waits for. Otherwise it is closed.
- [ ] `git status` is clean, and nothing is stashed.
- [ ] I am back on `main`, pulled, and `Scripts/check` is green.
- [ ] The issue says what was done and what was not.
- [ ] Whatever waits on the owner is one pull request he can approve. Nothing else asks for him.
- [ ] He was told in three lines what was run and what it showed.
