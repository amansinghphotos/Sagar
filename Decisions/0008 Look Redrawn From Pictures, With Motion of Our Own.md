# 8. Look Redrawn From Pictures, With Motion of Our Own

Decided by the owner on 27 November 2026. It ends the copying that decision 7 allowed.

**Context.** Under decision 7, Look's motion and then its parts were taken from Search word for word,
with Search's notice kept. The owner then saw each part drawn twice, once copied and once from Search's
pictures alone, and the two matched. Motion is the part a hand feels most.

**Decision.** Nothing of Search's is pasted. Each part is rewritten from Search's pictures. Its motion
is written here from how Search's moves, read for ideas, and kept within two hundredths of Search's
at every moment, measured against a record of Search's own. When the last copied line is gone, Search's
notice leaves the acknowledgements and the About window, and a check keeps it from returning. Look is
still built whole, ahead of need, as decision 7 allows.

**Consequences.** Each copied file is replaced in its own slice, proven by the pictures and motion
records it already matches; the notice stays until the last.
